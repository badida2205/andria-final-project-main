Chakapuli

Chakapuli – A Classic Georgian Spring Dish Chakapuli is a traditional Georgian stew, typically made with lamb or veal, fresh herbs, and green sour plums (known as tkemali). It is especially popular in the spring when ingredients like tarragon, green onions, and plums are in season. The dish is slow-cooked, allowing the meat to become tender and absorb the tangy, herbal flavors. Chakapuli is known for its bright, refreshing taste and is a staple of Georgian festive tables, celebrating the arrival of spring with every bite.
