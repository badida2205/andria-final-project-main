Gebzhalia

Gebzhalia – A Unique Dish from Western Georgia Gebzhalia is a traditional dish from the Samegrelo region of western Georgia. It is made with fresh, soft cheese (usually made from curd or mozzarella-like cheese), mixed with a creamy sauce of matsoni (a type of Georgian yogurt), mint, and sometimes garlic. The cheese is typically rolled or sliced and then served cold, making it a refreshing appetizer. Gebzhalia is known for its delicate texture and the unique combination of dairy and aromatic herbs, showcasing the rich culinary heritage of the Megrelian people.
