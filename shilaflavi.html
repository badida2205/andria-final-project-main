Shilaplavi

Shilaplavi – A Traditional Georgian Rice Dish Shilaplavi is a traditional Georgian rice dish, often prepared with meat such as beef or lamb, and flavored with onions, garlic, and various spices. The rice is simmered in a rich meat broth, allowing it to absorb deep, savory flavors. Sometimes, tomatoes or herbs are added depending on the regional variation. Shilaplavi is typically served as a main course and is known for its hearty, comforting taste. It is often enjoyed during family gatherings or festive meals, reflecting the warmth of Georgian home cooking.
