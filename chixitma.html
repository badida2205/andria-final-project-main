Chikhirtma

Chikhirtma – A Traditional Georgian Soup Chikhirtma is a traditional Georgian soup, especially popular during the colder months and often considered a hangover remedy. It is made with boiled chicken, a rich broth, and a mixture of eggs and lemon juice, which gives the soup its signature creamy texture and slightly tangy flavor. Unlike many other soups, chikhirtma is thickened without using flour or cream, relying instead on the egg-lemon mixture for richness. This comforting dish reflects the simplicity and depth of Georgian home cooking.
