Nazuki – A Sweet Georgian Bread Nazuki is a traditional Georgian sweet bread, especially popular in the town of Surami. It is made from a spiced dough flavored with cinnamon, cloves, and sometimes raisins, then baked in a clay oven (tone) to give it a slightly crispy crust and soft, fragrant interior. Nazuki is often enjoyed as a snack or dessert and is commonly sold along roadsides, especially in central Georgia. Its warm aroma and rich flavor make it a beloved treat for both locals and visitors.
