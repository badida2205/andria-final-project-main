Mtsvadi

Mtsvadi — a traditional dish of Caucasian and Asian peoples. It is traditionally made from lamb or pork — skewered on metal or wooden sticks. This dish has a long history of preparation.

Mtsvadi is considered one of the earliest thermally processed dishes. Roasting meat in ash or embers is a relatively simple method for humans, and as a result, almost every country has its own version of mtsvadi, with nearly every nation naming a similar dish among its traditional cuisine. Nevertheless, it is still regarded as a traditional dish of the Caucasian and Asian peoples.
