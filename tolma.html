Tolma

tolma – A Stuffed Dish Shared Across Cultures tolma is a popular dish in Georgian cuisine, consisting of grape leaves stuffed with a flavorful mixture of minced meat, rice, onions, and herbs. Though variations of dolma exist across the Caucasus and the Middle East, the Georgian version often includes a blend of spices and is typically served with a tangy garlic-yogurt sauce. It can also be made vegetarian, using rice and herbs alone. Dolma is commonly enjoyed during family meals and festive occasions, symbolizing hospitality and shared culinary heritage in the region.
