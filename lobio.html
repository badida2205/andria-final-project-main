Lobio in a Clay Pot

Lobio in a Clay Pot – A Rustic Georgian Favorite Lobio, meaning “beans” in Georgian, is a beloved traditional dish made with red kidney beans, herbs, and spices. One of the most authentic and flavorful versions is lobio cooked in a clay pot, which enhances the earthy taste of the ingredients. The beans are slow-cooked with onions, garlic, coriander, and sometimes walnuts or chili for added depth. Served hot, often with mchadi (cornbread) and pickled vegetables, this hearty dish is a staple of Georgian village and tavern cuisine, reflecting the country's rustic culinary charm.
