Khashi

Khash – A Traditional Georgian Broth Khash is a traditional Georgian dish made by slowly boiling cow’s feet, tripe, and other parts until they turn into a rich, gelatinous broth. It is typically eaten early in the morning, especially during cold seasons, and is often considered a remedy for hangovers. Khash is usually served plain, with garlic, salt, and vinegar added by the diner to taste. It is commonly accompanied by lavashi (Georgian flatbread) and vodka or chacha. Though simple in ingredients, khash holds a strong place in Georgian food culture and social gatherings.
