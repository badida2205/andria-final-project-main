Kutia

Kutia – A Traditional Ritual Dish Kutia is a traditional ritual dish made from boiled wheat grains, sweetened with honey and mixed with ground walnuts, raisins, and sometimes poppy seeds. While it is commonly associated with Eastern Slavic countries as a Christmas Eve dish, in Georgia, it is primarily prepared for memorial meals (kelekhi) to honor the deceased. Symbolizing the cycle of life and death, the wheat represents rebirth, and the sweetness of honey reflects the hope for peace in the afterlife. Kutia is served cold and plays a meaningful role in Georgian spiritual and culinary traditions.
